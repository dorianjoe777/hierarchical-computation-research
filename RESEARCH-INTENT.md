# Original research intention and scope

Clarified by the user on 10 September 2026. Conceptual source: [@dorian_x10](https://x.com/dorian_x10). This is an assistant-written record of the intended hypothesis and research direction, not a statement that the hypothesis has been established.

## The intended viewpoint

The starting point is the user's idea of our own characteristic level of reality and scale: what can be observed, what counts as motion or an operation, and how time is experienced or measured may depend on a system's position within a larger organization.

Internal feedback is central. The user proposes that interacting components maintain a form of internal coherence or “boundness,” and that the same organization may constrain access to fine details from another level. Collective consequences—the proposed “skeleton”—then participate in a larger level, where the relationship between internal activity and accessible results recurs.

The quantum connection is part of this original intention. The hypothesis is that a corresponding limitation or form of uncertainty could occur at each scale, with what we call quantum uncertainty being one possible manifestation from our own position. The user intends this relationship to extend through a proposed eleven-dimensional structure. The mathematical meaning of those dimensions, their relationship to organizational levels, and the proposed propagation between them remain to be specified and justified.

The association between internal feedback and scale-relative uncertainty belongs to the user's hypothesis. Earlier AI statements went beyond it when they presented the association as an already proved information barrier or an established explanation of quantum uncertainty. Correcting that evidential overstatement must not erase the intended association from the research.

## Core and boundary applications

The core question concerns the relationship between internal feedback, coherent organization, temporal structure, and the constrained but usable information passed between levels. The proposed recurrence of this relationship across levels is part of the question to develop mathematically.

The human–AI discussion in the original transcript is an optional downstream consequence. It is not a central premise, an early experiment, or a reason to choose the initial model. The same discipline applies to other speculative consequences: investigate the common proposed mechanism first, then consider applications only where a result supports them.

Quantum uncertainty and quantum/relativistic descriptions retain their explicitly intended role as medium- to long-term physical applications. Philosophical and cosmological conclusions remain boundary interpretations. None of these applications establishes the core mechanism in advance.

## How this intention governs the work

The user's subsequent manuscript makes asymmetry, potential, and completion central to the immediate investigation. It proposes a “carga-tiempo” relation, fixed underlying adjacency with internal state motion, an internal RAM-like resource or mode, reciprocal influence between local and collective clocks, and continuous exchange that can appear as a stable external object. These terms must receive operational mathematical meanings before being equated with physical quantities.

The proposed time relation may concern gross internal energy exchange rather than net total energy change. Both readings must be distinguished. The manuscript also uses different frequency intuitions for internal and higher-level observers; no universal faster/slower ordering has been established. Observation bandwidth, sampling, and integration must be specified separately.

The eleven-level completion/reset picture and possible cosmic identity of RAM remain intended hypotheses. The initial model must account for finite resources, reciprocal interactions, dissipation, and any reset work; writing a level index or assuming a cycle does not derive the physical proposal. The [manuscript preservation record](manuscripts/README.md) retains the latest wording separately from these interpretations.

The user subsequently described an ultimate self-reference interpretation: the whole attempts to certify its own nonexistence, but the act leaves a boundary or residual that renews activity, proposed as the source of time and existence. This interpretation is preserved in the [self-reference frontier](self-reference-frontier-2026-09-10.md). The user explicitly asks that the visualization be translated into mathematical and physical counterparts now, rather than remain only a philosophical discussion. The [working model v0.1](models/feedback_null_v01/SPEC.md) begins that translation with distinct global/local zero states and fully specified dynamics. Its defined null state is not absolute nonexistence.

The research order is foundations, then examination of their limits, then applications supported by the findings. The [foundations overview](FOUNDATIONS-OVERVIEW.md) consolidates the current state. Physical self-measurement belongs in this work; the human–AI boundary discussion remains peripheral.

The medium- to long-term physical objective is to investigate whether a rigorously developed mechanism could provide a starting point toward understanding uncertainty across levels and its relationship to quantum physics and relativistic descriptions. A first successful model would motivate further research in that direction; it would not establish the entire proposed structure.

The immediate work should address a necessary precursor: under explicitly stated dynamics and observation rules, can internal feedback produce both a coherent effective unit and a quantifiable limitation on accessible detail? Identify its effective variables and characteristic time, and distinguish a physical limitation from information deliberately discarded by an analyst.

The existing finite-state prediction benchmark is a calibration tool for part of that question. It does not define the full research intention, and its novelty assessment cannot by itself settle the originality or validity of the broader proposed mechanism.

Different claims need separate evidence:

1. A coarse description is sufficient for a selected prediction.
2. A specified observer has limited access to microscopic detail under fixed resources and allowed interactions.
3. A common mechanism relates such limits across defined levels.
4. That mechanism reproduces the quantitative physical uncertainty being targeted.
5. The proposed dimensional structure follows from, or is supported by, the model.

Progress on one claim does not establish the next. This order preserves the user's physical motivation while making the route toward it testable.
