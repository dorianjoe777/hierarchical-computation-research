## 1. The intended perspective

In the user's picture, the entire hierarchy attempts to establish its own nonexistence or emptiness. The act of establishing that condition produces activity, a boundary, or a residual record. That residue prevents final self-certification and renews the attempt. What appears as existence and time would be the continuing consequence of this unresolved process. The same pattern is proposed to recur within and between levels, with apparent completion renewing asymmetry through feedback.

This retains the manuscript's philosophical direction: the proposed process is not simply an ordinary system working toward an externally chosen goal. The user intends it as an account of why any ongoing system or temporal activity would appear at all. Mathematical models below can investigate limited analogues of that account; they already assume a state space and rules, and therefore cannot by themselves explain why there is something rather than nothing.

The words “attempt,” “prove,” and “understand” express the interpretation. A physical realization would need a rule, controller, or coupling implementing the corresponding behavior. No intention, consciousness, or universal optimizer follows from the vocabulary.

## 2. Keep emptiness, a record of emptiness, and failure to detect separate

An empty set, a zero signal, a quiescent physical state, an incomplete description, and the absence of any system are different concepts. A mathematical empty set belongs to a formal theory; a physical detector returning zero is an outcome under a measurement model. Neither establishes absolute nonexistence.

The record “nothing was detected” is part of the detector's state. The existence of that record does not imply that its represented target was present. A contradiction can arise if the target is instead defined to include the record and to prohibit any record, but then the incompatibility follows from that particular specification.

This yields an important scope distinction: a system may successfully certify the absence of a specified excitation while being unable to satisfy a specification requiring both a persistent certificate and no physical certificate-bearing state. Failure of that stronger task does not force a new universe into existence. The scientific problem is to state the target and the role of the measurement record before discussing impossibility.

| Manuscript concept | Candidate operational translation | Remaining gap |
|---|---|---|
| Emptiness / non-state | A specified null set, such as absence of a selected excitation | The model already contains states and a measurement procedure |
| Fullness / completion | A target set, exhaustive prediction of selected observables, or resource exhaustion | These are distinct meanings; none is automatically equivalent to emptiness |
| Self-reference | A physical record and controller belong to the composite system they describe | Some self-descriptions are consistent and tractable |
| Phantom or mask layer | A measurable boundary response or record generated by interaction | A metaphorical boundary is not yet a spatial surface or field |
| Renewed asymmetry | A defined residual or displacement regenerated by an interaction | Prediction error, thermodynamic imbalance and quantum uncertainty are different quantities |
| Time as failed measurement | A proposed relationship between unresolved self-measurement and recurring physical events | Event ordering, clock rate and time's existence require separate arguments |
| Unseen energy | A resource omitted from the internal observer's description | Inaccessibility of a source does not make it absent from the physical energy balance |

These are candidate translations, not claims that the manuscript has already selected unique scientific definitions.

## 3. Separate three propositions

**Logical proposition:** some self-involving specification cannot be satisfied under stated rules.

**Dynamical proposition:** a physical implementation of a specified measurement/control task cannot settle, or settles only under particular conditions.

**Temporal proposition:** its recurring events provide a clock, or more ambitiously account for temporal structure.

Each needs additional assumptions. A logical inconsistency has no automatic evolution law, and a nonconvergent calculation has no automatic physical rate or energy source. A recurring process can be a clock without explaining the existence of time.

For an elementary logical control, let a publicly announced binary prediction \(p\) forecast an action \(a\), and impose the response \(a=1-p\). Universal correctness for that protocol would require

\[
p=a=1-p,\qquad p\in\{0,1\},
\]

which has no solution. This demonstrates an incompatible prediction-and-response requirement. It does not show that every self-measurement is impossible. It also does not imply the dynamical rule \(p_{n+1}=1-p_n\); that rule is a separate choice.

Three maps make the distinction explicit:

- \(m_{n+1}=m_n\) has fixed states.
- \(m_{n+1}=\alpha m_n\), \(m_n\in\mathbb R\), \(0<\alpha<1\), converges to zero.
- \(m_{n+1}=1-m_n\), \(m_n\in\{0,1\}\), alternates with period two.

All feed a state into its subsequent update. Their different outcomes come from their rules and domains; feedback alone does not select oscillation. The Boolean period-two map already assumes update events. Assigning each update a duration would introduce a clock convention rather than derive one.

[Wolpert's *Physical limits of inference*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.1362) is directly relevant prior work: it formalizes inference devices and proves limits under defined inference tasks. It motivates careful treatment of an observer inside the world it predicts. Its results do not identify logical limits with quantum uncertainty, produce energy, or establish the origin of time. The small example above is an elementary illustration, not a reproduction of all of Wolpert's theorems.

## 4. A candidate experiment connecting the frontier to the existing core

After the initial interacting-observer model is established, include an explicit finite record/controller state \(m\) in the modeled composite. Let \(x\) describe the physical subsystem and \(r\) its accounted resource. A schematic discrete organization is

\[
\begin{aligned}
y_n &= h(x_n,m_n,r_n)+\nu_n,\\
m_{n+1} &= G(m_n,y_n),\\
x_{n+1} &= F(x_n,m_n,r_n),\\
r_{n+1} &= R(x_n,m_n,r_n).
\end{aligned}
\]

These functions are placeholders for a physically justified joint update; they are not derived from the philosophical hypothesis. The measurement, control and resource backaction must be included in that update. Noise \(\nu_n\) is either supplied by a declared physical model or omitted in the deterministic baseline. Begin with a finite target and a finite memory, not unrestricted universal self-prediction.

For a target observable \(\chi\), define the record's forecast \(\widehat\chi_n=D(m_n)\), the exact target time, and a loss comparing it with \(\chi\) of the relevant joint state. Compare two tasks separately: describing a subsystem property, and describing a property that includes the changing measurement record. Any inference limitation must name which task it concerns.

Choose a nonnegative completion residual \(A(x,m,r)\), defined independently of the outcome sought. In a continuous realization with physical law \(\dot w=f(w)\), calculate

\[
\frac{dA}{dt}=\nabla A\cdot f(w),\qquad w=(x,m,r).
\]

Then test whether measurement/control regenerates a residual that another part of the dynamics reduces. Do not insert a compulsory reset or a positive residual floor and subsequently claim to have discovered unavoidable incompletion. Record whether the loop has fixed points, attraction to a target, transient oscillations, sustained cycles, or irregular behavior, and what assumptions change the outcome.

The candidate scientific question is:

> Under specified physical dynamics and resources, can attempting to certify a system's own completion regenerate a measurable discrepancy through the measurement/control interaction, thereby sustaining activity or limiting an accessible description?

This addresses a limited part of the philosophical proposal. A positive result would establish a feedback mechanism under its assumptions. It would not establish that nonexistence causes existence.

Required comparisons include disabling the causal return of the measurement record while accounting for any remaining detector load; using a declared replayed control with comparable resources; targeting a property that excludes the record; and changing memory, measurement strength, delay, and noise separately. Compare powered operation with finite-resource depletion. Ordinary delay-induced oscillations or imposed toggling must be identified as such.

## 5. Energy, recurrence and time remain separate tests

Include the system, observer, controller, memory and resource in an energy account. The deterministic port-Hamiltonian balance already in the [mathematical scope](mathematical-plan-feedback-energy-clocks.md) remains applicable under its assumptions. Work from control, time-dependent couplings, and memory handling cannot be supplied by the label “self-reference.”

[Sagawa and Ueda's feedback thermodynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.3262) supplies a relevant framework for relating measurement information and feedback in stochastic systems. It changes how information enters thermodynamic relations, not the need to account for the apparatus and resources. Any irreversible record-reset claim needs its specified physical protocol and thermodynamic assumptions; there is no universal energy price for an arbitrary abstract logical operation.

The proposed chain must therefore be examined link by link:

\[
\text{specified self-measurement}
\ \longrightarrow?\ \text{regenerated residual}
\ \longrightarrow?\ \text{persistent physical events}
\ \longrightarrow?\ \text{usable clock}.
\]

The question marks matter. If events disappear when supplied power is exhausted, that is a resource-dependent clock mechanism. If a contradiction simply prevents a specification from being met, it is a logical limitation. Neither outcome alone establishes a cosmic origin of time.

Using \(n\) or \(t\) to investigate a process already supplies ordering or temporal dynamics. A later proposal for time from a more primitive description must identify its assumptions and derive operational temporal correlations without presenting that preexisting parameter as an output. Whether an arrow of time follows is another question requiring directional statistical or thermodynamic structure.

Proceed with the bounded self-measurement extension if the basic model gives enough control over records, interactions, and resource costs to distinguish genuine feedback effects from prescribed cycling. Accept convergence, finite exhaustion, or a successful self-description as informative outcomes. Restrict any impossibility claim to the demonstrated class.

The metaphysical statement that existence is a recurrent failure to establish nonexistence remains an interpretation. Present evidence does not establish it or assign it a probability. Its scientific value will depend on whether a defined consequence differs from existing models and survives mathematical and empirical checks.
