# Foundations overview and research order

**Consolidated 10 September 2026.** Conceptual source: [@dorian_x10](https://x.com/dorian_x10). This is the current overview of the research's intentions, definitions, assumptions, evidence, and next steps. Completing this overview does not mean the proposed mechanism or physical theory has been established.

## The research question

Can the physical interactions that form and maintain a collective unit also establish its clock behavior and determine which internal details another interacting system can access? Can a rigorously demonstrated relation of this kind recur across organizational levels?

The user's initial perspective includes fixed underlying relationships with internal motion, coherent units participating in a larger organization, “carga-tiempo,” an internal RAM-like resource, scale-relative uncertainty, and an intended eleven-level structure. The latest philosophical extension proposes that attempts at complete self-certification perpetually renew the activity experienced as time and existence. The [research intention](RESEARCH-INTENT.md) and [manuscript preservation records](manuscripts/README.md) retain these motivations separately from derived claims.

Human–AI applications remain peripheral. Physical self-measurement is relevant on its own and does not require a cognitive or conscious observer.

## What the existing calculations establish

The two-mode oscillator baseline has an explicit energy balance and normal modes. Conservative coupling changes the differential-mode frequency without removing its excitation. Specified selective damping can suppress that mode while an initially excited undamped common mode survives. These are elementary deductions from chosen dynamics.

A nonzero oscillator can keep moving while total energy remains constant. This rules out net total-energy change as a universal measure of clock progression in that model; gross internal exchange remains a distinct candidate to test. An undriven full-state periodic cycle cannot dissipate positive net energy under the stated autonomous balance.

An ideal continuous noiseless single-site observation can reconstruct the coupled pair's state when coupling is nonzero and parameters are known. This demonstrates why feedback cannot simply be assumed to protect all detail. A real finite-bandwidth interacting probe requires a separate calculation.

The [rational audit](audit/check_energy_clock_controls.py) checks selected exact identities and reconstruction formulas. Working model v0.1 adds four deterministic numerical controls, with energy-budget and step-refinement checks. Neither is a noisy-observer experiment, physical validation, a novelty demonstration, or a proof of the complete hypothesis. The earlier finite-state prediction controls remain available as independent calibration examples.

## The sequence: foundations, limits, applications

### A. Establish the foundation model

Freeze the full pair/resource/probe specification, units, initialization, damping, power inputs, target, and observation channel. Derive its response and energy accounting. Define the coherence measure and allowed observer protocols before comparing information access. Distinguish a resource mode from an established higher-level collective clock.

**Exit criterion:** the model is inspectable and internally consistent, elementary controls are reproduced, and the central experiment has fixed assumptions and a clear failure condition. The overview and first executable analogue are now assembled. The four-preparation information task is specified in v0.1; its execution and the broader resource-matched comparison remain ahead. An autonomous logical certification controller requires an explicit extension.

### B. Examine its limits

Test vanishing and strong coupling, bandwidth and duration limits, finite resources, probe backaction, fixed energy versus fixed signal amplitude, stable collective behavior versus averaging, and erased detail versus difficult-to-access detail. Establish when the model converges, oscillates, or fails to maintain organization.

Then include an explicit record/controller for a bounded self-measurement task. Compare targets that include and exclude that record. Ask whether the act of certification regenerates a residual, whether it creates a resource-dependent cycle, and whether a different admissible protocol removes the effect. A logical inconsistency, noisy prediction, chaotic motion and quantum uncertainty require different explanations.

Derive a two-level effective model only when a candidate collective unit has been identified. Keep approximation errors, memory and energy bookkeeping explicit. No existing definition requires exactly eleven levels.

**Exit criterion:** a conditional characterization, useful counterexample, or bound survives the relevant controls. Any claim of unavoidable limitation must cover the specified observer/model class; a single failed implementation is insufficient. Physical validation may be used here when a realizable apparatus can discriminate competing explanations.

### C. Develop applications from established conclusions

Possible early applications include sensor design, synchronization with finite resources, reliable collective-state readout, or reduced models with explicit error and observation costs. These are candidate directions, not achieved benefits. Select one only when an actual result supports it.

Quantum and relativistic work needs the additional structures listed in the physical assessment. Philosophical origin claims, cosmic RAM, and eleven-level reset scenarios require their own derivations and observable consequences. They cannot supply missing evidence for the core experiment.

**Exit criterion:** an application has a defined task and observable advantage or explanatory result relative to the closest established method, under comparable resources.

## Rules for interpreting progress

- Preserve the original wording independently of all scientific translations.
- Label definitions and modeling choices before deriving their consequences.
- Specify whether a limitation concerns prediction, measurement, control, energetic resources, logical consistency, or the chosen representation.
- Keep the controller and observer inside the energy and causal account when they are physically inside the system.
- Treat unresolved energy sources as missing model components; lack of access is not evidence of energy from nothing.
- Derive temporal observables from a model without claiming that an assumed update index or time parameter has thereby been explained.
- Allow negative findings to narrow the hypothesis; compare positive findings with prior work before asserting novelty.
