## What is being tested

For a small physically specified system, can the interactions that maintain a coherent collective unit also produce a measurable, resource-dependent restriction on access to its internal details? Which parts follow from dynamics, and which depend on the chosen observer or assumptions?

The relationship may be selective or have the opposite sign. More coupling can improve observation. “Asymmetry,” “potential,” and “completion” receive separate mathematical definitions before being identified with any energy, force, or transition. The question of what drives interactions is addressed through a declared energy function, reciprocal couplings, dissipation, and explicit power sources.

The scope supplies elementary analytical derivations. The [audit script](audit/check_energy_clock_controls.py) performs exact rational spot checks of the energy/mode identities and observability formulas. These checks are not the full resource/probe experiment or a claim of novelty.

**Completion:** a model specification and analytical response with all assumptions explicit, and no hidden energy supply or inaccessible-variable oracle presented as an ordinary observer.

## Then test time and access together

**Next executable research task:** run the fully specified four-preparation likelihood experiment in [v0.1 §6](models/feedback_null_v01/SPEC.md#6-a-completely-specified-first-information-task-ready-for-a-separate-run). It fixes the target, initial energy, times, noise and decoder. Use that as the baseline for the broader resource-matched comparisons below; do not interpret this idealized final readout channel as a fundamental detector limit.

Freeze a four-preparation internal-phase target and a probe protocol family. Define the observation duration, sampling, integration window, bandwidth, noise, coupling, work, and permitted disturbance. Measure achieved information about that same target, organization before and during observation, and energy transfer.

Compare fixed energy with fixed signal amplitude. Distinguish modes erased by damping from modes remaining present but hard to observe. Distinguish persistent collective behavior from averaging or aliasing. A finite family of successful decoders cannot prove an upper bound on all physically accessible information.

**Continue if:** a conditional relation survives the controls, or a counterexample gives a sharper, useful hypothesis.

**Revise if:** the effect is caused only by chosen averaging, sensor cancellation, noise mismatch, changed target, resource depletion, or an assumed synchronization law. Report such outcomes directly rather than reinterpreting them as quantum evidence.
